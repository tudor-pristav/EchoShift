# How to play Echo Shift

## Start a profile

Open **Login** and use demo player `test` / `test` or `test1` / `test`. To create a new profile, open **Admin Login**, sign in with `admin` / `test`, and choose **Create Account**. Return to the main menu and log in as the new player.

From Player Home, select a level and confirm it to begin. **New Game** resets your highest unlocked level after confirmation; it does not immediately launch a round.

## Survive the shift

A night lasts six real minutes, from midnight to 6 AM. You start with three lives. The Entity moves through the factory toward your office; reaching it costs a life. Losing all lives ends the round. Surviving unlocks the next night, up to Night 3.

1. Read the word at the bottom of the screen.
2. Type it correctly to scan and reveal the Entity for two seconds.
3. To lure it, first click a room on the map, then type a complete word. The lure only moves the Entity if that room is adjacent to its current room.
4. Watch your lives, score, and the clock.

Uppercase and lowercase letters work equally. A wrong letter fails the word and penalizes your score; after a short pause, a new word appears. There is no backspace correction for a failed word.

## Power-ups and shop

Buy power-ups from Player Home's shop before starting a shift. Click their icons during play:

| Power-up | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Extra Life | Adds one life. |
| Easier Words | Switches subsequent words to the easy bank for the remainder of the shift. |
| Instant Lure | Returns the Entity to its starting room immediately. |

Counts appear beside the icons. Coins are awarded from your round score when the shift ends.

## Finish and return

The round saves statistics and remaining power-ups when it ends. Click **Return to Player Home** to visit the shop, review statistics, or select another night. Closing the window mid-shift saves the interrupted round but does not unlock the next level.

## Settings and administration

Settings controls audio volume. High Scores displays locally stored player scores; there is no online leaderboard.

**Admin Login** accepts the included demo credentials `admin` / `test`. The admin panel can inspect player statistics and delete player accounts. Deleting a player removes their statistics and power-up inventory.

Accounts and passwords are stored locally in plaintext as part of the original course design. Use passwords made only for this demo.
